# Koala Facts

- **Scientific name:** *Phascolarctos cinereus*
- **Type:** Marsupial (not a bear, despite the nickname "koala bear")
- **Native to:** Eastern and southern coastal Australia
- **Diet:** Almost exclusively eucalyptus leaves
- **Sleep:** 18–22 hours per day
- **Digestion:** A long cecum and specialized gut bacteria detoxify eucalyptus oils
- **Water:** Rarely drinks — gets most moisture from leaves; the name is thought to derive from a Dharug word meaning "no drink"
- **Paws:** Two opposable thumbs on the front paws for gripping branches
- **Fingerprints:** Koalas have fingerprints that closely resemble human ones
- **Young:** Babies are called joeys; gestation is about 35 days, then ~6 months in the pouch
- **Senses:** Poor eyesight; relies mainly on smell and hearing
- **Lifespan:** Around 10–15 years in the wild
- **Conservation status:** Vulnerable (habitat loss, disease, and climate change)
